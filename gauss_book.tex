% Preface
\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
\markboth{Preface}{Preface}

Carl Friedrich Gauss (1777--1855), often called the ``Prince of Mathematicians,'' left an indelible mark on virtually every branch of mathematics. From his teenage discovery of the construction of the regular heptadecagon to his monumental \textit{Disquisitiones Arithmeticae}, from the method of least squares to the fundamental theorem of algebra, from Gaussian elimination to the Gaussian distribution---his contributions form the bedrock of modern mathematics.

This book takes a computational approach to exploring Gauss's mathematics. Rather than merely presenting theorems and proofs, we implement Gauss's algorithms, reproduce his calculations, and extend his ideas using Python. Each chapter focuses on a major area of Gauss's work, providing:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Historical context and mathematical background
    \item Rigorous mathematical development with proofs
    \item Python implementations of Gauss's algorithms
    \item Numerical experiments and visualizations
    \item Exercises for deeper exploration
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Who this book is for:} Students of mathematics, computer science, and physics; researchers interested in computational number theory, numerical analysis, and mathematical history; and anyone who wants to experience Gauss's mathematics through the lens of modern computation.

\textbf{Prerequisites:} Basic knowledge of Python programming, linear algebra, calculus, and elementary number theory. Familiarity with \texttt{numpy}, \texttt{scipy}, and \texttt{matplotlib} is helpful but not required---we introduce these tools as needed.

\textbf{Structure of the book:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Part I: Number Theory} (Chapters 1--4) covers modular arithmetic, quadratic reciprocity, Gauss sums, and the \textit{Disquisitiones Arithmeticae}.
    \item \textbf{Part II: Analysis and Special Functions} (Chapters 5--7) explores the Gaussian integral, theta functions, and the arithmetic-geometric mean.
    \item \textbf{Part III: Linear Algebra and Numerical Methods} (Chapters 8--10) covers Gaussian elimination, least squares, and numerical integration.
    \item \textbf{Part IV: Statistics and Probability} (Chapters 11--12) examines the normal distribution, error analysis, and Gaussian processes.
    \item \textbf{Part V: Geometry and Astronomy} (Chapters 13--15) investigates the heptadecagon, differential geometry, and orbital mechanics.
\end{itemize}

Each chapter includes a companion Python module in the \texttt{python/} directory, with thorough documentation and tests in the \texttt{tests/} directory. A separate \textit{Student User Guide} provides step-by-step tutorials for running the code, modifying experiments, and extending the implementations.

\textbf{Acknowledgments:} We are grateful for the digitized works of Gauss available through the G\"ottingen Digital Library, the comprehensive commentary in \cite{gauss-works}, and the modern expositions in \cite{edwards-fermat, stillwell-number-theory, cohen-number-theory}.
