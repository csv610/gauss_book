\section{Exercise Solutions}

\begin{solution}
Gauss's method for orbit determination uses three observations $(\alpha_i, \delta_i, t_i)$ to find the heliocentric positions $\mathbf{r}_i$. From the f and g functions, $\mathbf{r}_2 = f_1\mathbf{r}_1 + f_3\mathbf{r}_3$ gives a linear system for the unknown distances. The semi-major axis follows from the vis-viva equation $v^2 = \mu(2/r - 1/a)$.
\end{solution}
